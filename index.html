Cost questions

How much does a skylight cost in Toronto?

Residential skylight installations in Toronto typically run $1,400–$4,500 fully installed. The range depends on type (fixed vs vented vs solar), roof (sloped vs flat), and whether a new opening is being cut. See the full cost guide on the main site for line-item breakdown.

Why are quotes so different?

Three quotes for the same skylight can swing $1,500. Reason: different installers spec different units, different flashing kits, and different prep work. Always ask quotes to specify product brand and model, the flashing kit included, and whether ice-and-water shield extends around the curb.

Does my insurance cover skylight damage?

Storm damage (hail, windstorm, fallen tree) is usually covered. Age-related failure isn't. Maintenance-related failure usually isn't. Document storm damage immediately with photos — most policies require prompt notification.

Installation questions

How long does installation take?

A typical residential install is 1–2 days — half a day to cut and frame the opening, the other half to install, flash, and finish drywall. See the installation process guide on this site for the day-by-day breakdown.

Do I need a permit?

For a direct replacement in an existing opening — usually not. For a new cut, especially in a load-bearing rafter — yes. New skylight installation services handle the permit application as part of project scope.

Will it leak?

A properly installed skylight with manufacturer flashing kit and proper ice-and-water shield should not leak for 20+ years. About 80% of "leaking skylight" calls are actually flashing failures, not unit failures.

What about during winter?

Skylights perform well in Toronto winter when installed correctly. Some condensation on cold mornings is normal. Heavy or persistent condensation indicates a ventilation issue, not a skylight problem.

Product questions

Which brand should I choose?

Velux and Fakro are the two main brands. Velux has wider parts availability and the better smart-home ecosystem (Velux Active). Fakro tends to be 15–25% cheaper. Both are excellent products.

Fixed vs vented?

Fixed for stairwells, hallways, rooms without humidity concerns. Vented (manual or electric) for bathrooms, kitchens, anywhere moisture builds up. Vented adds $400–1,200 to the cost depending on operator type.

What about sun tunnels?

Sun tunnels are smaller, simpler, and cheaper ($900–1,800 installed) than traditional skylights. They work great in closets, hallways, and small interior rooms where you can't justify the cost of a full skylight. See sun tunnel skylights.

Can I add a skylight to a flat roof?

Yes — but the installation method is completely different. Flat-roof skylights sit on built curbs with proper membrane termination. Costs are higher ($2,200–4,800). See flat-roof skylight options.

Repair vs replace questions

My skylight is leaking — repair or replace?

If the unit is under 15 years and the damage is localized → repair. Over 20 years, multiple leak points, or rotted curb → replace. See the typical scope at skylight repair or skylight replacement.

What's emergency repair for a leaking skylight?

If water is actively dripping during a storm, that's emergency. Emergency skylight repairs dispatch 24/7 across the GTA for tarping and temporary stabilization, with permanent repair scheduled once the weather breaks.

Why are my glass panes fogged up?

Condensation between the panes (not on the surface) means the IGU seal has failed. Glass replacement is possible on most modern Velux and Fakro units without replacing the whole skylight.
